Rane SL3 CoreAudio Driver

Native for Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4) & Intel

Original idea and project by: pepelee (PPLEE)
Instagram: @pepeleedj — instagram.com/pepeleedj/

This installer sets up the low-latency driver for the Rane SL3 audio interface on your Mac:

  • 6 inputs and 6 outputs (Aux, Left Deck and Right Deck) at 24-bit / 44.1 or 48 kHz, with a low-latency audio engine.
  • Automatic USB / Thru switching when you open and close your audio software (Ableton Live, Traktor, Mixxx, Serato, Logic Pro, etc.).
  • SL 3 Control Panel app (in Applications) and the sl3panel utility: each deck's Phono/Line switch, Thru, phono sensitivity, volume and levels.
  • Serato DJ Pro 2.5.5: includes Sl3Api.framework (DVS modes ABS / REL / INT and THRU).
  • Works straight away: no restart needed, and no need to turn off System Integrity Protection (SIP).
